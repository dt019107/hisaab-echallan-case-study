## 📖 The Core Story

In India, when a commercial delivery truck or taxi breaks a traffic rule on a highway, the police camera tags the fine to the vehicle's registration plate on the government portal (**Parivahan VAHAN**) — not the driver.

For commercial fleets and leasing companies managing hundreds of vehicles with rotating driver shifts, this creates operational chaos:
1. **Traffic fines attach to trucks, not people**: When tickets arrive 3 weeks later, managers have no easy way to know who had the keys at that exact hour.
2. **Expired deadlines cause compounding penalties**: Unorganized spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages lead to missed 15-day dispute windows, doubling fine amounts.
3. **Blacklisted road permits**: Unpaid tickets cause the government to freeze vehicle registration renewals, halting business operations.

### How Hisaab Solves It:
- **Instant GPS Matching**: Checks the exact minute of the violation against GPS trip logs and driver shift rosters, attributing blame in seconds with **98.4% accuracy**.
- **Separated Payer & Party Architecture**: The fleet company immediately pays the fine from escrow so the truck stays legal on the road, then automatically recovers the money from the driver's next paycheck or adds it to the corporate client's monthly lease invoice.
- **Zero Late Fees**: Automated SLA countdowns ensure zero missed deadlines and complete financial reconciliation.

## 🖥️ The Six Core Interactive Production Screens

1. **Screen 01 • Executive Dashboard**: Real-time compliance KPIs, ₹18.4L outstanding liability tracker, regional corridor deadline rulers, and prioritised triage work queue.
2. **Screen 02 • Vehicle Listing & Ingestion**: 842 active commercial assets, VAHAN real-time synchronization, and multi-channel CSV/Excel column mapping wizard.
3. **Screen 03 • Vehicle Custody Deep-Dive**: Sub-minute driver shift custody timeline, compliance credentials (RC, Insurance, PUCC, Fastag), and violation alignment.

5. **Screen 05 • Challan Details & AI Attribution**: Dual-column layout with ANPR speed camera radar photo telemetry, Clause 7.2 AI matching engine, and driver payroll deduction picker.
6. **Screen 06 • Multi-Party Settlement & Resolution**: Dual-ledger financial reconciliation, official Parivahan clearance voucher with digital seal, and 4-stage audit trail.

## 🎨 Design System & Anti-AI-Slop Standards

- **Single Typographic Hierarchy**: Headings set in authoritative **Archivo**, body in **Inter**, telemetry in **JetBrains Mono**. Zero decorative italic serif intrusions.
- **Calibrated Cobalt Brand Token (`#1E51D4`)**: Signature enterprise blue with 4% brand-tinted neutral scale (`#F0F4FA`, `#C8D5E8`, `#0D1B2E`).
- **High-Contrast Surfaces (WCAG AAA)**: Pure white cards with 1px solid borders. Zero frosted glass cards or unreadable low-contrast grays.
- **Authentic Framing**: Direct product interface and real photographs under calibrated scrims. Zero fake macOS traffic-light window chrome.
