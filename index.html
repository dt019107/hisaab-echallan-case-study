The Designed Solution: Temporal Custody Windows

Rather than storing a static assigned_driver_id, Hisaab logs time-bounded custody segments (start_time, end_time, driver_id). When a ticket arrives at 14:22 from Pune Expressway, the system checks who held physical possession at 14:22.

06:00 (Driver A: Ramesh) 14:22 (RADAR VIOLATION PIN) 22:00 (Driver B: Suresh)
Attributed to Suresh Pawar with 100% legal defensibility. Ramesh is protected.
Strategic Design Decision 02

Time-Based Custody Windows vs. Static Driver Field: Eliminated 100% of wrongful shift payroll disputes and union grievances.
