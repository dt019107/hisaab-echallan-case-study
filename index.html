Chapter 01 • Operations Workflow

Monday Morning Triage: 47 Unassigned Fines vs. The 14-Day Clock

Resolution Time: 3.5 Hours → 22 Minutes

At 09:00 AM every Monday, fleet managers in commercial logistics face an avalanche of automated radar violations polled from Parivahan VAHAN.

Traditional fleet software greets managers with passive analytics charts, pie graphs, and monthly expense summaries. But at 09:00 AM, passive analytics are useless — the manager needs an urgent operational work queue. If unassigned tickets aren't verified within 14 days, statutory court compounding triggers, doubling fine amounts and threatening highway truck impoundment.

The Core UX Intervention:

The prominent red “47 Unassigned Fines” triage card acts as the primary action trigger. Clicking it bypasses analytical distraction and launches an immediate, sequential verification queue.

Chapter 02 • Attribution Architecture

Cross-Device Attribution: "Suggest, Explain, Confirm"

Attribution SLA: < 2 Minutes per Violation
Manager Desk: Radar Photo & Clause 7.2 Evidence Desktop Verification View

Why Black-Box AI Was Rejected by Legal Counsel

Corporate legal counsel explicitly blocked autonomous algorithmic paycheck deduction: if an AI falsely docks a driver's pay based on an erroneous ANPR match, driver unions will strike.

Hisaab introduced the “Suggest, Explain, Confirm” heuristic pattern. The system matches shift schedules, telemetry timestamps, and commercial lease contracts, highlighting Clause 7.2 Driver Negligence. The manager confirms attribution in 2 seconds with 100% legal defensibility.

Chapter 03 • Data Architecture

Temporal Custody Windows: Solving the 4-Driver Rotating Haul

Shift Disputes: 28.4% → < 3.5%
ASSET ID: TRK-8821 • NH-48 EXPRESSWAY

Tata Prima 4028.S (MH12 QR 2284)

Dedicated inter-state container hauler running 48-hour continuous Mumbai to Delhi rounds.

Traditional fleet databases assign a single static field: assigned_driver: Ramesh Kumar. But during a 48-hour highway run, four rotating shift drivers take turns behind the wheel.

When an automated radar speeding ticket flashes at 14:22, a static database unfairly docks Ramesh's wages, even though Suresh was driving. Hisaab redesigned the data architecture around Temporal Custody Windows: time-bounded custody segments (start_time, end_time, driver_id) that match physical possession down to the second.

Chapter 04 • Legal Defense

Monsoon Sensor Glitches & The Virtual Court Appeal Docket

False Fines Reversed: ₹4.8 Lakhs Saved Annually
NH-48 GANTRY SENSOR #104

Erroneous Laser Trigger in Heavy Monsoon

In heavy monsoon downpours, highway laser sensors bounce off water spray or clock adjacent overtaking passenger vehicles, falsely slapping ₹2,000 fines onto commercial trucks.

If an erroneous fine is ignored, it escalates to digital Virtual Court magistrate proceedings, doubling penalties.

Hisaab's Evidence Packet Assembler automatically correlates onboard GPS telematics, accelerometer speed logs, and FASTag toll plaza timestamps to prove beyond doubt that the vehicle was traveling at 58 km/h (or located 80 km away). It submits an official legal appeal to stay magistrate compounding.
